Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to make an appointment?

While we do accept walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to secure your preferred time and technician.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and cash. Gratuity can be added to your card or paid in cash directly to your technician.

Do you offer group bookings or parties?

Yes! We love hosting bridal parties, birthdays, and girls' days out. Please call us directly to arrange a group booking so we can accommodate your party perfectly.

How long do acrylic extensions typically last?

With proper care, our acrylic extensions can last 3-4 weeks before needing a fill. We recommend regular maintenance every 2-3 weeks to keep them looking flawless.
