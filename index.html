Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Do I need an appointment?
While we do accept walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to guarantee your preferred time and technician.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), and Apple Pay.
Do you offer group bookings or parties?
Yes! We love hosting bridal parties, birthdays, and girls' days out. Please call us directly to arrange group accommodations.
How do you sanitize your tools?
We adhere to strict medical-grade sterilization processes. All metal tools are sanitized in an autoclave, and files/buffers are single-use and discarded after each client.
